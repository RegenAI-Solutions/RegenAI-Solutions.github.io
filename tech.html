The Foundation: DayCent Model Credibility

The DayCent model, developed over 40 years by Colorado State University (CSU), is a globally recognized tool for simulating ecosystem processes, including carbon cycling and GHG emissions. Its credibility is validated by its extensive use in peer-reviewed scientific literature, making it a preferred choice for robust environmental research.

To secure our technological foundation, RegenAI Solutions is finalizing a **non-exclusive license agreement with CSU** for the worldwide usage of the DayCent model, granting us unrestricted access to its core code and ongoing developments for our commercial applications.

The DayCent-AI Ecosystem

DayCent-Client

A core Python library enabling MMRV providers to programmatically automate entire project workflows, from preparing inputs to calculating ERR, PMU, and model bias, crucial for managing large-scale projects with minimal intervention.

DayCentAPI

A serverless AWS Lambda architecture that enables massive parallelization of DayCent runs, capable of processing millions of simulations concurrently. It's ideal for managing grouped projects and ensures a cost-effective, scalable solution.

Voiceara (Forthcoming)

A mobile app using LLMs to ingest farm data from any format—paper records, voice notes, or Excel files. It automates QA/QC and converts unstructured data into model-ready configurations, removing technical barriers for farmers.

Model Calibration (DayCent-PEST)

Our direct integration of PEST and PEST++ automates parameter estimation and uncertainty analysis, enabling us to generate higher-quality, scientifically robust carbon credits that command a premium price.

Proprietary Algorithms

Custom algorithms for spatially balanced sampling and multi-pass stratification ensure our field data is efficient and representative, minimizing model uncertainty and maximizing credit issuance for farmers and investors.

DayCentGUI

A professional graphical interface that consolidates all project files into a single workbook. This streamlines QA/QC and provides a single, auditable source of truth for technical experts and verifiers.

A Foundation of Data Quality

To ensure our data and reporting meet the highest standards, we have developed a comprehensive set of **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)**. These SOPs govern our entire MMRV framework, providing a clear, traceable, and registry-compliant methodology for soil data collection, scientific data extraction, and quantification workflows.
