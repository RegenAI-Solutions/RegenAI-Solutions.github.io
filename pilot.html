A New Approach: The Automated MMRV Pilot

Our proposed $160,000 pilot project directly tackles the most expensive and time-consuming phase—MMRV—by leveraging the DayCent-AI Suite. The goal is to prove a scalable, cost-effective model for Vietnam.

Traditional Approach 👎

  • Manual Data Processing: Relies on expensive, expert-level labor.
  • High Consulting Fees: Data integrity services can exceed $100k.
  • Complex & Slow: Manual work consumes 80-90% of a modeler's time.
  • High Barrier to Entry: Prohibitive costs prevent local scalability.

Agro-Carbon Catalyst Pilot 👍

  • Automated Workflow: DayCent-AI automates data processing.
  • Reduced Costs: Aims to drastically lower MMRV expenses.
  • Fast & Efficient: Frees up experts to focus on analysis, not formatting.
  • Scalable Model: Creates a replicable framework for Vietnam.
